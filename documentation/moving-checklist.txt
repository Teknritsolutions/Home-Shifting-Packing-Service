MOVENEST — A LITTLE CLARITY FOR MOVING DAY

Two to four weeks before
[ ] Compare written moving scopes and estimates.
[ ] Build a room-by-room inventory.
[ ] Confirm building access and preferred dates.
[ ] Set aside fragile or specialist belongings.

The week before
[ ] Sort, donate and pack nonessential items.
[ ] Label each box with room and contents.
[ ] Arrange utility changes with your providers.
[ ] Keep essential documents and valuables with you.

Moving day
[ ] Pack medicines, chargers, toiletries and first-night essentials.
[ ] Walk through your inventory with the crew.
[ ] Point out fragile items and access restrictions.
[ ] Check every room before handing over keys.

At your new home
[ ] Check delivered items against the inventory.
[ ] Place boxes in the right rooms.
[ ] Unpack essentials first.
[ ] Take a moment to enjoy your new beginning.

General demo guidance. Confirm specific requirements with your moving provider.
