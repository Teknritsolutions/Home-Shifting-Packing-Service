The MoveNest guide

A clear guide to
your moving website.

Everything you need to run the site, adjust its design and understand how the booking workspace works.

In this guide

Start here Make it yours Bookings & invoices Theme & direction Before going live Helpful downloads
01 / Get started

Open the website locally.

The website uses standalone HTML, CSS and JavaScript with locally stored images and fonts. No compilation is required to view the delivered pages. 

npm start

Open http://127.0.0.1:4173 in your browser. Keep the folder structure intact so the images, fonts and shared styles resolve correctly.

02 / Make it yours

One consistent design system.

Colors, spacing, typography and shared controls live in the assets/css folder. The main body text uses a readable 16px size; secondary captions, labels and links have a clear supporting hierarchy.

  • Brand: edit the shared mark and wordmark, then regenerate the logo and favicon assets.
  • Content: edit a delivered HTML file, or change the shared builder and regenerate the site.
  • Images: use descriptive filenames and meaningful alternative text.
  • Shared layouts: the header and footer are generated consistently across the site.

Running the builder overwrites generated HTML. Keep your edits in either the delivered pages or the builder so your preferred source stays clear.

03 / Your workspace

Follow one move from start to finish.

Use Dashboards in the main header to open the customer or admin workspace. A submitted booking, its tracking milestones and its invoice share the same browser-local record.

  • Customers can create a booking, inspect the estimate, follow milestones and print an invoice.
  • Admins can review bookings, assign crews, adjust statuses and manage inquiry records.
  • The pricing calculator uses editable illustrative rates. It does not issue a confirmed commercial quotation.
  • Reset controls restore the original sample records and settings.

This is a browser-local experience. Passwords are not stored, no payment is collected, and no external email or live dispatch request is sent. Sample customer portraits and stories are illustrative.

04 / Choose your view

Light, dark and your reading direction.

The appearance controls sit together in the header. The moon switches to dark mode; the sun switches back to light mode. The adjacent RTL or LTR control changes the layout direction and remembers your choice.

Colors, form fields, tables, menus and chart labels follow the selected theme. Numerical chart plotting retains its normal direction for readability.

05 / Before going live

Connect the services your business needs.

For a live commercial service, connect a secure backend for identities, authorization, bookings, messages and persistent shared records. Use a real payment provider and the applicable tax-invoice rules before accepting payments.

Replace sample contact details, company figures and customer content with your own verified information. Update the example domain in metadata, the sitemap and robots.txt before publishing.
