A little extra care for your fragile favourites

A practical guide to giving your most delicate belongings a safer journey.

Useful advice to put into practice
A little knowledge goes a long way.

Some belongings are more than objects. They’re the mugs from slow Sunday mornings, the vase from a favourite trip, the plates that always make it to the family table. Give those things a little extra time before moving day.

Start with a thoughtful sort

Set fragile pieces aside before packing the rest of the room. Check their condition, measure unusually shaped items and decide what needs its own box.

Choose the right materials

  • Strong double-wall boxes in manageable sizes
  • Clean packing paper and honeycomb wrap
  • Cardboard dividers for glasses and cups
  • Paper tape and a clearly readable marker
Good packing fills the empty space around your belongings, while keeping pressure off the belongings themselves.

Wrap each item separately

Cushion the bottom of your box. Wrap each piece on its own and separate hard surfaces. Plates travel best on their edges with padding between them. Avoid making a single box too heavy to handle comfortably.

Label for a gentler arrival

Write the destination room, a short contents list and a visible fragile note on at least two sides. Keep a quick inventory so unpacking becomes a simple room-by-room process.

A final check before you close

Fill gaps with paper so items stay still. Close the lid without force, tape the seams and keep valuable personal documents with you. Ask your moving provider about specialist packing for artwork, mirrors and unusual items.
